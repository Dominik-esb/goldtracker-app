What the 10 km in 1:10 says

  • 7:00 /km at 178 bpm for 70 minutes. Holding that means your max HR is at least 192; zones use a working max of 195.
  • That was about 91 % of max, a race effort. Equivalents today: 5 km ≈ 33:00, half ≈ 2:35.
  • Engine (resting HR 52, HRV 57) is well ahead of running economy. Economy responds to volume and easy running, not intensity.

What it changes in the plan

  • No run-walk phase. Week 1 starts at 20 km over 3 runs. Four runs from week 7, five from week 17.
  • Easy means under 156 bpm. Expect 7:30 to 8:00 /km and walking hills for about six weeks. This is the biggest change.
  • Sleep is the limiter. Oura's two lowest readiness inputs are sleep balance (66) and sleep regularity (69). Target 7.5 h, bed by 00:30 before key days.
  • Strength twice a week from week 1. It was regular in June (7 sessions) and faded (5 since). Main injury insurance at 89 kg.
  • Log runs in Oura with the ring on. Without them the readiness rule has no load to react to.

8. Race week

  • Carbohydrate load from Thursday: 8 to 10 g per kg body weight per day, low fibre on Saturday.
  • Race morning: breakfast 3 h before, 60 to 90 g carbs, caffeine 60 min before if you are used to it.
  • Pacing: first 5 km at goal pace plus 5 s/km, middle 10 km at goal pace, last 6 km whatever is left. A positive split over 2 min means you went out too fast.
  • Fuel: one gel at 45 min and one at 75 min, water at every station in warm weather.
